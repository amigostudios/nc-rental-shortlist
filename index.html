Before you call
  • Ask about air conditioning on the first call. Coastal Carlsbad houses often have none, and most listings simply do not mention cooling. "AC not stated" in this list means no source said either way, not that it has none.
  • Solar: ask whether the panels are owned or leased and who gets the credit. With leased panels the tenant usually pays the monthly lease on top of the electric bill, so the saving can be small.
  • Garage: ask whether it is actually usable. A few of these have an owner's storage area, a converted room, or a tandem layout that fits one car in practice.
  • Dog policy is informational now, but still ask about pet deposit and monthly pet rent before applying; they add $25 to $75 a month and a few hundred up front at most of these.
  • Good Carlsbad houses under $4,600 go in days. Have pay stubs, ID and a credit report ready so you can apply the same day you tour.
  • Scam check for by-owner listings: never pay a deposit before seeing the inside with keys in hand, and match the owner's name against the San Diego County assessor's parcel record (free lookup).
