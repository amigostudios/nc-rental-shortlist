What we looked for, and how to read this
  • 3 or more bedrooms, a real garage, a dog allowed, rent at or under about $4,600
  • Near Calavera Hills (Carlsbad 92010) or San Elijo Hills (San Marcos 92078). Distances are straight-line miles to each.
  • Every listing below was re-checked on Oct 13 against a source we could read directly (the manager's own site, the MLS feed, Craigslist, Apartments.com, Zillow or Trulia). Anything marked no pets, no garage, or already rented was cut; those are listed at the end.
Top picks come first, then detached houses that are a step behind, then townhomes with 2-car garages. "Small dogs only" is the listing's own wording; none of them publish a weight cutoff, so one phone call with the dog's breed and weight settles it. Prices are base rent unless noted.

Before you call
  • Dog size decides about half of this list. Call with the dog's breed and weight first; none of the "small dogs" listings publish a cutoff, and three of them (Hampton Rd, Durango Way, Saratoga Ct) contradict themselves between the text and the pet field.
  • Expect a deposit equal to one month, a $35 to $55 application fee per adult, 2.5x to 3x income, and 620+ to 700+ credit. Where pet terms were published they ran $250 to $500 one-time and $25 to $100 a month.
  • Houses near Calavera Hills under $4,600 are almost all in Oceanside 92056 on the Carlsbad line (Tri-City), and several are still zoned for Carlsbad schools. Inside San Elijo Hills itself, only one detached house (Chert Dr) and two townhomes (Cattail Ct, Caprise Dr) were in budget on Oct 13.
  • Move quickly on Mira Costa, both Lake Blvd houses and Genine Dr: all are available now and priced at or under their rent estimates. Bryan St already has an application in process.
  • Several homes are listed only on Zillow with no phone number. Use Request a tour, and ask who owns or manages the home before paying any fee. Never pay a deposit before seeing the inside in person.
  • Prices moved during the day this was researched (three cuts on Oct 10 to 13). Re-open the links before calling; a listing that is gone was probably rented, not pulled.
